# ModularHub Europe — logo assets

## Current direction: v4 „Passage”

The user-selected flat identity is stored in `v4/`. Use its horizontal primary lockup on light surfaces, the reversed lockup over the client homepage hero, and the app icon for browser/application identity. The website implementation uses the same geometry through `components/brand/BrandLogo.tsx`, keeping the Manrope wordmark native and responsive.

V2 and v3 below remain archived direction history and should not be introduced into new screens.

These files explore the relationship between the **Clearance Gate** symbol and the working title “Modular Hub Europe”. They are not final trademarks or production wordmarks.

## Primary display direction: v3

The `v3/display/` folder contains the effect-rich display logo based on the architectural depth and lighting level of the supplied inspiration. It is intended for large, high-impact contexts:

- website hero sections;
- trade-show walls and presentation covers;
- launch visuals and large-format signage;
- brand-direction reviews.

File: `v3/display/logo-modularhub-display-v3.png` — 1536 × 1024 px.

The v3 display logo contains depth, material lighting and a warm-lit glass detail. It is the primary expressive face of the current brand direction. Do not use it as a favicon, compliance stamp, PDF footer, embroidery or one-colour print. Use the flat v2 symbol and lockups for those applications. Full rules are documented in `docs/brand-guidelines-v3.md`.

## Operational direction: v2

## Editing

- Replace only text nodes when testing a new name; keep symbol geometry unchanged.
- Preferred typeface: IBM Plex Sans Condensed 600/500.
- Fallbacks in the SVG remain editable and are not converted to outlines.
- Before production delivery, convert the approved final wordmark to vector outlines using the licensed font files.
- Full-colour palette: Deep Navy `#10263D`, Passage Blue `#175CD3`, Slate Note `#566473`.
- Reversed version: solid white on Deep Navy or Passage Blue.
- Monochrome version: one solid colour; remove the blue datum rule or recolour it to match.

## Important

The current name is a working title. Do not register, print at scale or deploy these lockups publicly until naming, linguistic and trademark checks are complete.
